# Project scope

## In scope

- Student registration, college-email verification, login, logout, password reset, and profiles.
- Product listings with search, filters, sort, wishlist, and seller trust signals.
- Purchase requests with accept, reject, cancel, simulated payment, and dispute.
- Auctions with reserve prices, bid history, anti-sniping, and server-side closing.
- A service catalogue limited to tutoring, study help, and creative work.
- Product exchanges, direct chat, in-app notifications, reviews, reports, and an admin dashboard.
- Rule-based price suggestions, campus deals, watchlists, recently viewed items, and category recommendations.

## Out of scope

- Real payment gateways, wallets, or payouts.
- Native mobile apps.
- Email delivery infrastructure beyond the labelled local demo token.
- Proctoring, plagiarism detection, or any workflow whose purpose is completing graded work for another student.
- Multi-campus tenancy and production SSO.
