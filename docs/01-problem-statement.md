# Problem statement

Students accumulate textbooks, hostel items, and skills that other students on the same campus need. Open classifieds mix in the general public, hide whether a seller is actually a student, and do not handle bidding or exchanges well.

CampusKart gives a college a single place where a verified student can sell, auction, exchange, or offer legitimate help, and where the college can suspend abuse. The prototype demonstrates those workflows with a real API and database. It does not process real payments and it does not help students submit graded work for each other.
